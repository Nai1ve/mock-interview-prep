## Predicted Questions

### 🔴 High Probability (7 questions — must prep 100%)

**Q1: Walk me through your experience with AI/LLM-powered content production.**
- **What they're really asking**: Can you actually ship AI-augmented content at scale, or is this just prompt tinkering?
- **Situation**: At a US-based remote startup, the blog had zero content and no production pipeline.
- **Task**: Build an end-to-end AI content production system that could ship 10+ technical articles per month.
- **Action**: Built multi-agent Claude pipelines (research → draft → review → image generation → compliance scan). 15+ specialized skills, 77 automation scripts.
- **Result**: End-to-end publishing time went from hours to a single CLI command. 68 articles in ~3 months.
- **Key data**: 15+ skills, 77 scripts, 68 articles, hours → CLI command.
- **Pitfall**: Don't say "I used ChatGPT to write articles." Emphasize the system design and quality gates.
- **Follow-up if pressed**: "What was the hardest quality problem?" → answer: maintaining technical accuracy at scale; solved with multi-pass verification.
