# Company context

Use company context only when it materially changes the assessment: role scope, product constraints, collaboration structure, regulatory requirements or interview format. Confirm evidence from the supplied JD or current reliable sources. Company size or brand alone does not establish a specific interview process.

A small team may emphasize autonomy and breadth; a larger team may emphasize ownership boundaries and collaboration. These are hypotheses to adapt to the actual role, not rules. Do not assert that a company always asks a question, requires a language, or has a particular current strategy without evidence.

Use this reference only when company context is relevant; it does not override resume/JD coverage, timing or evaluation criteria. Reverse-question suggestions can help the candidate clarify unknown scope, success criteria and team constraints after the simulation. Do not manufacture employer answers.
