# Question Bank — General Interview Question Framework

> This is a **question-type framework**, not an exhaustive bank. At skill runtime, pick the relevant archetypes by JD + resume template, then fill specifics from the candidate's actual material.
> Do not pre-fill answers — answers are generated at runtime against the current JD/resume.

## A. Self-introduction

**A1. 1-minute self-introduction** (HR screen always)
- Standard structure: identity positioning (1 sentence) + what you did in the past X years (2-3 sentences of headline experience) + what you're looking for next (1 sentence)

**A2. What are your core strengths / describe yourself in 3 keywords**
- Do not go generic ("diligent / fast learner")
- Be specific: [capability X] + [evidence: project Y, quantified result Z]

**A3. What's the project you're proudest of**
- This is bait — your answer reveals how you define "achievement" (execution? strategy? team?)
- Pick one: non-trivial setting + clear ownership + strong quantified result

## B. Why-leaving / why-applying

**B1. Why are you leaving / why aren't you staying**
- ❌ Don't bad-mouth previous company / boss / team
- ✅ Frame: previous role gave me [X experience] → now I want [Y, next growth stage] → your company / this role offers [Z]

**B2. Why this role / why us**
- ❌ "Your business is great" / "I'm interested in this industry" — cliché
- ✅ Specific product/strategy/team at the company + 1 specific sentence on how your capability creates value

**B3. What's your salary expectation**
- Don't say "depends on the company" / "negotiable" — that's a signal HR uses against you
- Give a range + an anchor (based on [previous salary / market level / your core deliverable])

## C. Resume project deep-dive (hiring manager always)

**C1. Pick a project from your resume and walk me through it**
- Prepare 3 depths: 30s → 2min → 10min
- Use STAR: Situation - Task - Action - Result
- **Every quantified number must have methodology behind it** (not just "lifted 30%" but "why 30%, how it was measured, the next phase will be at 50%")

**C2. What was the biggest challenge of this project, how did you solve it**
- Pick a "real" challenge (not generic "long hours" / "shifting requirements")
- Be clear: "I made decision X because of Y" (decision replay)

**C3. If you were redoing this, what would you change**
- ❌ "I think we did it well, nothing to change" — reads as not self-aware
- ✅ Acknowledge 1-2 specific trade-offs / regrets (don't disown the outcome but show growth)

**C4. What's your ownership vs the team's contribution**
- Tests whether you take team credit
- ✅ Clear: "I owned X, teammate A did Y, overall the ownership was mine"

## D. Behavioural (STAR)

**D1. Have you had a conflict / disagreement with a colleague, how did you handle it**
- Wrong answers: "I always defer" / "I always hold my ground" — both wrong
- ✅ Frame: disagreed with [role] on [topic] → my POV X, theirs Y → drove decision via [data / user / executive call] → outcome

**D2. Have you failed / made a mistake**
- Must give a specific example (don't say "I haven't had a major failure")
- Pick "medium" — not catastrophic (no business impact) but with a real lesson learned
- Emphasise: how fast you recognised the mistake + how fast you fixed + systemic prevention

**D3. How do you handle pressure / multiple priorities**
- ❌ "I work well under pressure" — abstract
- ✅ Concrete method: [Eisenhower / time blocking / saying no to X type of request] + a recent specific scenario

**D4. How do you report to stakeholders / drive cross-team collaboration**
- Tests whether you're a closed-door IC
- ✅ Concrete: [weekly / monthly / sync] cadence + key stakeholders + last time you drove [project] step-by-step

## E. Domain depth

**E1. What's the biggest trend in this space right now**
- Don't say "AI is important" — generic
- ✅ Specific to [sub-domain]: [specific technology / product form / business model change]

**E2. What do you think of our product / business — what could be improved**
- Killer question — tests whether you really prepared
- ✅ Must pre-research the company: used their product / read competitor comparisons / give 2-3 specific optimization ideas

**E3. If you were tasked with [project the JD implies], how would your first week / month start**
- Tests hands-on decomposition
- ✅ Frame: first [research / understand existing state] → list [hypotheses] → use [fastest validation method] → decide next step

## F. Values / cultural fit (final round)

**F1. What does success mean to you / what do you want to do in 5 years**
- Honest but directional (don't say "I don't know")
- Don't describe "founding my own company as CEO" — reads as not grounded
- ✅ Moderately specific: in [domain] reach [role level] + want to solve [problem]

**F2. What have you read / learned recently**
- Tests lifelong-learning habit
- ✅ 1-2 books / 1-2 podcasts / 1 domain you're deep-diving right now

**F3. What does a great [role] look like to you**
- Tests depth of role understanding
- ✅ Frame: core capability X + key trait Y + anti-example (what makes a [role] not great)

## G. JD risk-point defense

**G1. JD requires X years of experience, you have Y (Y < X)**
- ❌ "I can close this gap" — abstract
- ✅ "My Y years are denser than peers" + quantified evidence (independently shipped [N hard projects])

**G2. JD emphasises [industry] experience, you haven't worked in it**
- ✅ "I haven't done [industry] but I've done [adjacent X], the transferable skill is [Y]" + research you've already done (read N industry reports / used X competitor products)

**G3. JD requires technical skill X, your resume didn't emphasise it**
- ✅ Acknowledge + explain why the resume didn't emphasise it + show you have it (personal project / coursework)

## H. Reverse questions for HR

See [interview-stages.md](./interview-stages.md) for stage-specific reverse questions.

Core principle:
- **HR stage**: ask about team challenges / role success criteria
- **Hiring manager stage**: ask about business depth / roadmap / current team pain
- **Cross-functional stage**: ask about cross-team collaboration / current bottlenecks
- **Final stage**: ask about company strategy / organisational bottlenecks

## How the skill uses this file

At Step 2 (question prediction), the skill:
1. Reads the JD → extracts which dimensions the interviewer likely cares about
2. Reads the tailored resume → knows your strengths / weak points / quantified data
3. Picks 5-7 🔴 high-probability + 5-7 🟡 medium-probability questions across A-G
4. Fills concrete content for each (from your resume material, not generic)
5. Picks 3-5 H-type reverse questions by interview stage

**This file is not an answer bank** — it is "question types + answer frames". Each skill run fills concrete content against the specific JD/resume in context.
