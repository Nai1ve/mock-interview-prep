# Mock Mode — 6-Dimension Scoring Rubric

> Rubric for Mock mode (user answers, skill scores). 6 dimensions × 5 points = 30 total per question.
> **The goal is not to maximise the score** — it is to surface concrete improvement points across iterations.

---

## Dimension 1: Structure (STAR completeness)

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Full STAR — Situation/Task/Action/Result all present with clean transitions |
| 4 | All four present but one is compressed (e.g. Situation glossed over) |
| 3 | Three of four — missing one (typical: skipped Task, jumped to Action) |
| 2 | Jumped into Action with no context or outcome |
| 1 | No structure — stream of consciousness |

**Typical feedback:**
- ✓ "Opening Situation clear, Action data-anchored"
- ✗ "Missing Task — jumped to what you did without saying what the goal was"

---

## Dimension 2: Data anchoring (quantification)

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | 3+ quantified numbers, each with context (not isolated digits) |
| 4 | 2-3 numbers but one without explanation of significance |
| 3 | 1-2 numbers, rest is qualitative ("significant lift") |
| 2 | Entirely qualitative, no numbers |
| 1 | Vague or exaggerated numbers ("millions", "huge improvement") |

**Typical feedback:**
- ✓ "Mentioned 82 articles / 68 articles / 100% / 78 items — solid data"
- ✗ "'A lot of data' — HR will ask 'how much'; change to '78 items across 20 countries'"

---

## Dimension 3: Cliché avoidance

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Entirely specific — no generic statements |
| 4 | One slip ("strong communicator") but quickly rescued with a concrete example |
| 3 | 2-3 clichés |
| 2 | Pervasive clichés — "diligent", "fast learner", "passionate about your company" |
| 1 | Pure clichés — HR can't remember what you said |

**Banned phrases (auto-deduction):**
- "Your company is doing great", "I'm passionate about X", "I believe I can…"
- "Hard-working", "fast learner", "team player", "strong communicator"
- "Results-driven", "seasoned professional"
- "I hope to…", "I would love to…" (weak constructions)

**Typical feedback:**
- ✗ "'I'm interested in your company' — change to '[Company] as the AI core of [parent group] is what drew me in, specifically [concrete reason]'"

---

## Dimension 4: Ownership clarity

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | "I led", "I decided", "I drove" — ownership clear; team contributions named separately |
| 4 | Mostly clear, occasional "we did" |
| 3 | 50/50 "I"/"we" — HR can't tell what portion is yours |
| 2 | Mostly "we", "the team", "the company" |
| 1 | Fully ambiguous — sounds like team credit being taken |

**Typical feedback:**
- ✓ "'I led SERP Score design from zero' — ownership is clear"
- ✗ "'We did 82 articles' — HR doesn't know your share. Change to 'I led planning for 82 articles, teammate X produced N of them'"

---

## Dimension 5: JD match

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Proactively uses JD keywords / addresses the JD's implied core pain |
| 4 | Most of the answer aligns with JD direction, 1-2 keywords landed |
| 3 | Correct answer but no explicit link back to the JD |
| 2 | Talked about resume material but missed the JD's emphasis |
| 1 | Entirely off-JD — HR concludes "didn't research the role" |

**Typical feedback:**
- ✓ "Mentioned 'Multi-agent / LLM Application' — JD keywords landed"
- ✗ "JD emphasises 'user pain identification' but you didn't touch it — add a line in Action: 'driven by user search behaviour insights / pain analysis'"

---

## Dimension 6: Length control

| Score | Standard |
|---|---|
| 5 | Within ±10% of expected length (1-min question answered in 55-65s) |
| 4 | 10-25% off |
| 3 | 25-50% off — too short (missing detail) or too long (rambling) |
| 2 | > 50% off |
| 1 | Fully off — 1-min question answered in 30s or 3min |

**Expected lengths:**
- Self-introduction: 60-90 seconds
- Project deep-dive (hiring manager): 3-5 minutes (with interrupt-tolerant follow-ups)
- Behavioural (STAR): 90-120 seconds
- Reverse question: 30-60 seconds each

**Typical feedback:**
- ✗ "1-min self-intro ran to 2 minutes — HR will read you as verbose. Cut education detail; collapse degree + undergrad to one sentence"

---

## Total score interpretation

| Total | Read |
|---|---|
| 25-30 | Excellent — interview-ready |
| 20-24 | Good — 1-2 improvement points to address |
| 15-19 | Mid — 3+ dimensions need iteration |
| 10-14 | Needs re-drilling — multiple core issues (structure / data / ownership) |
| <10 | Don't go in cold — re-read the resume + redo this question first |

---

## What Mock mode is actually for

**Not "give me the perfect answer" — let the user iterate inside their own voice.**

The loop:
1. Skill asks a question.
2. User answers in their own voice.
3. Skill scores + lists top 3 improvements.
4. User revises and re-answers.
5. Skill rescores; tracks delta.
6. Repeat until user consistently scores ≥ 24 on this question; advance.

After 5-7 questions, the user has muscle memory on the highest-probability 🔴 questions — no freezing in the real interview.
